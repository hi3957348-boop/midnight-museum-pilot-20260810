SCIENCE KEY · CONDENSATION

The Water Did Not Come from the Glass

Warm, humid air contains invisible water vapor. When the air touches a cold surface, the vapor cools and changes into tiny liquid water droplets. This process is called condensation.

Warm, humid air+Cold surface→Water droplets
